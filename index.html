02 / RESEARCHResearch focus

Position-Dependent Divergence in Pathogenic Outcomes: Comprehensive Multi-Tool Analysis of Valine-to-Methionine Substitutions in ZNF668

My BS thesis asks whether the same substitution causes different harm depending on where it sits in the protein.

  1. SimulateGROMACS molecular dynamics to assess conformational stability at each variant position.
  2. DockAutoDock Vina to predict binding affinity for each variant.
  3. ScoreMM-GBSA free-energy changes to compare variants.
  4. ValidateqPCR, digital PCR and Sanger sequencing to confirm predictions in the lab.
